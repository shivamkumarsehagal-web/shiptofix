Ship-to GSTIN errors: 2323, 5002, 2325, 3039, 5001 — what they mean, and how to fix them before the e-way bill

When the mandate returns, these five error codes are the rejections your invoices will hit. Under the announced rules, the pattern is the same in every case: the portal checks your ship-to details at the moment you generate the IRN or the e-way bill. By then the invoice is already written. Checking the file before that step is the only point where a mistake is still cheap to correct.

Error 5002 — E-way bill rejected 5002: Ship-to GSTIN is missing

What it means: Ship-to details (name and address) are present on the invoice, but the Ship-to GSTIN field is empty. Under the announced rules, that field becomes mandatory whenever ship-to details are given — and the e-way bill is rejected when it is empty.

Why it happens: The invoice was raised the old way — bill-to details filled, ship-to address typed in, ship-to GSTIN left blank because it was optional before. It also happens when the billing software defaults the ship-to GSTIN to blank for a delivery address that is not the buyer's own premises.

Fix: Enter the GSTIN of the place where the goods are actually delivered — the consignee's GSTIN for that location. If the consignee is unregistered, do not leave the field blank: enter URP (Unregistered Person). Blank fails. URP is accepted.

Error 2323 — Ship to GSTIN error 2323: Bill-to and Ship-to GSTIN are the same

What it means: In a bill-to / ship-to transaction, the Bill-to GSTIN and the Ship-to GSTIN must not be identical. Error 2323 is raised when both fields carry the same GSTIN.

Why it happens: The customer master defaults the buyer's GSTIN into both fields. The buyer is one party; the goods are delivered to a different party or a different registration of that party — a depot, a job site, a distributor's warehouse. The software copies the billing GSTIN across and the portal rejects it.

Fix: Put the delivery party's own GSTIN in the ship-to field. If the goods genuinely go to the same registration that is billed, it is not a bill-to / ship-to case — remove the separate ship-to details instead of duplicating the GSTIN.

Error 2325 — Ship-to state code does not match the Ship-to GSTIN

What it means: The first two digits of a GSTIN are the state code. Error 2325 is raised when the state in the ship-to address does not match the state code of the Ship-to GSTIN.

Why it happens: The GSTIN belongs to the consignee's registration in one state, but the delivery address is in another — common when a company has registrations in several states and the wrong one is picked from the master. It also happens with a stale address after a warehouse moves across a state border.

Fix: Use the GSTIN of the consignee's registration in the state where the goods are delivered. The address, the state, and the GSTIN must all point to the same place.

Error 3039 — Ship-to PIN code does not belong to the ship-to state

What it means: The PIN code in the ship-to address must fall inside the ship-to state. Error 3039 is raised when the PIN belongs to a different state than the one stated.

Why it happens: A copy-paste error in the delivery address, an old PIN after a relocation, or the head-office PIN carried into a branch delivery address. The state name gets corrected; the PIN does not.

Fix: Correct the PIN to the actual delivery location's PIN, and confirm the state field matches that PIN. Check the address against the consignee's own records, not against your last invoice to them.

Error 5001 — Ship-to GSTIN missing in the e-way bill generated from an IRN

What it means: When an e-way bill is generated from an existing IRN (invoice reference number), the ship-to GSTIN in the e-way bill request is mandatory under the announced rules. Error 5001 is raised when it is not provided in that step.

Why it happens: The invoice and IRN were created without complete ship-to details, and the person generating the e-way bill tries to complete them at dispatch — or the details exist in one system and not in the other.

Fix: Make sure the ship-to GSTIN is present when the e-way bill is generated from the IRN. If it was missing from the start, the gap has to be closed in the invoice data — see the IRN warning below, because under the announced rules, for B2B and SEZ transactions a ship-to GSTIN set at IRN stage cannot be changed afterwards.

The one mistake you cannot undo later

Under the announced rules, for B2B and SEZ transactions, the ship-to details set at IRN generation are carried into the e-way bill and cannot be amended at the e-way bill stage. A wrong Ship-to GSTIN entered at invoice stage is locked in. The correction then is not an edit — it is cancelling and redoing the document, with the vehicle waiting. This is why these five checks belong before the IRN, not after a rejection.

The URP rule, in one line

If the consignee is unregistered, enter URP in the Ship-to GSTIN field. Never leave it blank. Blank triggers error 5002; URP does not.

Questions people search for

Why was my e-way bill rejected with error 5002?

Under GSTN's announced ship-to rules, when ship-to details are given but the Ship-to GSTIN field is empty, the e-way bill is rejected with 5002. Those rules were due 1 August 2026 and are currently on hold — but the fix is the same either way: enter the consignee's GSTIN for the delivery location, or URP if the consignee is unregistered.

What is ship to GSTIN error 2323?

The Bill-to GSTIN and the Ship-to GSTIN on the invoice are identical. In a bill-to / ship-to transaction they must be different parties. Enter the delivery party's own GSTIN in the ship-to field.

What does error 2325 mean?

The state in the ship-to address does not match the state code (the first two digits) of the Ship-to GSTIN. Use the consignee's GSTIN registration from the state where the goods are delivered.

What does error 3039 mean?

The ship-to PIN code belongs to a different state than the ship-to state on the invoice. Correct the PIN to the delivery location's actual PIN.

What does error 5001 mean?

The e-way bill was generated from an IRN without the ship-to GSTIN in the e-way bill request, where it is mandatory under the announced rules. The ship-to GSTIN has to be supplied in that step.

Can I correct a wrong Ship-to GSTIN after the IRN is generated?

Under the announced rules, for B2B and SEZ transactions, no — ship-to details set at IRN stage cannot be amended when the e-way bill is generated from that IRN. The check has to happen before the IRN is created.

What if the consignee has no GSTIN?

Enter URP in the Ship-to GSTIN field. An unregistered consignee is a valid case; a blank field is not.
